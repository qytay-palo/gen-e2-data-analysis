**Executive Summary**
PS-003 computes year-on-year growth and compound annual growth rate (CAGR) from the clean workforce Parquet, then feeds those results into the live dashboard demo. For Demo 1, this matters because the analysis is not pre-baked: the Growth Trends tab is generated from current outputs and appears in the running PS-002 app as proof that the workflow can add fresh analytical views on demand.

**Key Growth Findings**
Using the overall "All" sector view in the growth rates Parquet, the top three profession CAGRs are physiotherapists at 7.70%, pharmacists at 6.96%, and doctors at 5.72%. Nurses follow closely at 5.65%, so growth is broad-based rather than concentrated in only one workforce group. The fastest YoY growth year in that overall profession view is 2015, when physiotherapists grew 11.12% year over year.

**Sector Insights**
Public remains the fastest-growing sector in the upstream EDA findings, and the PS-003 growth table supports that signal with the strongest public-sector CAGR appearing in pharmacists at 10.35%. Private also grows strongly for physiotherapists at 9.45%, but Public is the clearer headline sector for the demo narrative.

**Demo Role**
In the live demo, the new Growth Trends tab appears inside the existing PS-002 dashboard without a separate manual injection step. Stakeholders see a bar chart ranking CAGR by profession and a line chart of YoY growth over time, which turns the analysis into an immediately visible dashboard update rather than a static report.
